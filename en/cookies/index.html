Cookie policy

This English version is provided for convenience; the Romanian version prevails.

1. In short

This website does not use cookies.

We set no cookies of our own and use no analytics, advertising or social media plugins. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so your visit does not send data to third parties through such services.

2. Why there is no consent banner

The law requires consent for cookies that are not strictly necessary. As this website uses no cookies at all, there is nothing to ask consent for.

3. Other storage technologies

The website does not use other browser storage or tracking technologies, such as local storage or tracking pixels.

4. Changes

If we ever introduce cookies, we will update this page and, where the law requires it, ask for consent before setting them.

5. Company products

The websites and apps of the company's products have their own cookie policies.
